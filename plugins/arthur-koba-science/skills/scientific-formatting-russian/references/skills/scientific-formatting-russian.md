# Scientific Formatting — Russian Publication Requirements

Use only when a manuscript is submitted to a Russian journal, conference,
university or institution whose current instructions require Russian
publication conventions. Language alone does not establish jurisdiction:
an English-language submission can have Russian institutional requirements,
and a Russian-language text can target an international venue.

## Establish the applicable profile

1. Identify the submission venue, document type, language, accepted format
   and current official instructions. Record the issuing authority, date
   and edition of any referenced standard.
2. Separate binding requirements from optional templates, disciplinary
   conventions and examples. Apply specific GOST provisions only when
   relevant and required; never infer a universal Russian layout.
3. Resolve conflicts against the correct authority and submission stage.
   Escalate unresolved requirements before imposing a potentially
   incompatible style.

## Format and acceptance

- Apply specified title/author metadata, section order, bibliography,
  citations, typography, margins, equations, tables, captions, figure
  labels, panel letters and decimal conventions. Do not silently convert
  established units or technical symbols.
- Keep editable text, diagram and chart sources; regenerate accepted
  outputs and inspect the rendered DOCX/PDF/SVG at publication scale.
- Verify cross-references, numbering, citation metadata, table/figure
  placement, embedded fonts and equation integrity in the final file.

**Complete when:** each regional requirement is traceable to the actual
venue or standard and the output is checked against that profile.
If no target venue is given, describe available choices; do not guess
a regulatory or institutional template.
