# Scientific Formatting — Russian Venues (RU)

Use only when the requested manuscript, article, report or other scientific
work is explicitly intended for a Russian-language venue or an institution
whose applicable requirements call for Russian formatting conventions.
This is a **regional supplement** to Scientific Writing, not a global
publication standard and not a dissertation or thesis-writing workflow.

## Determine the actual authority

1. Identify the receiving journal, conference, university or institution,
   submission language, work type and current instructions. Obtain the
   official template and applicable standard, including edition and date.
2. Distinguish mandatory requirements from examples, local recommendations
   and traditions. Apply GOST references only when the receiving authority
   requires their relevant editions; never assume one GOST governs all
   Russian papers.
3. Record required typography, margins, headings, captions, numbering,
   bibliographic style, mathematical notation, figure/table placement and
   accepted export formats. Resolve conflicting instructions by their
   stated authority and submission scope; flag unresolved conflicts.

## Localize and verify

- Use Russian captions, axis/legend labels and diagram text when the
  requested deliverable is in Russian, preserving conventional symbols,
  units, accepted terms and proper names.
- Follow the specified local panel lettering and decimal separators.
  Do not impose Cyrillic panel labels or Russian terminology on
  international English-language documents.
- Maintain editable manuscript, diagram and chart sources; inspect
  generated DOCX/PDF/SVG at the required publication scale.
- Validate all cross-references, citation numbering, captions, units,
  formula symbols and bibliography against the target template.

**Completion:** the edition and origin of each applicable regional
requirement are known, and the final rendered files meet the selected
venue instructions. Without a venue, offer a configurable profile
instead of asserting universal Russian requirements.
